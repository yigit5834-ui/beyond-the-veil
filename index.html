Beyond the Veil

True stories from history that sound impossible.

Beyond the Veil is a YouTube channel about strange but real events: dancing plagues, ghost ships, vanished lighthouse keepers, and the myths that grew around them. Every story is researched from historical sources, and every video lists its sources and image credits.
